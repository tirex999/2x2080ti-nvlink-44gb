For a group where rotation.y=0 has forward as +x: move x += cos(a)*speed, z -= sin(a)*speed... Standard: forward vector = (cos(ry), 0, -sin(ry))? In three.js, with rotation.y=0 the object's +X axis is world +X. Rotating by ry around Y axis maps +X to (cos ry, 0, -sin ry). So target angle a = Math.atan2(-dz, dx)?
