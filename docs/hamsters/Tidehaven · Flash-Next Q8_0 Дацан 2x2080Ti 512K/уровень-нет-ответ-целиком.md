## What you're looking at

- **The cutaway lagoon** is the hero: a translucent turquoise water body with a wavy top surface (vertex-displaced, fresnel-lit, foam where it meets the shore), bright glass "aquarium" walls on the front and sides, additive caustics crawling over the sandy seabed, drifting bubbles, and a shoreline foam ring — all depth-sorted so nothing flickers.
- **The island** has a height-field terrain with sand→grass→deep color banding, instanced rocks and swaying grass, pines and palms, an A-frame cabin with a verdigris copper roof, glowing windows, a wooden deck with planks, string lights, towels, crates, a bench, potted plants, and a chimney.
- **The pier** runs out over the water with posts, railing, lanterns, a ladder into the lagoon, a mooring post, and a kayak.
- **Underwater life**: four rays with GPU-undulating fins/tails, three fish schools, swaying kelp, and coral — all kept inside the lagoon.

## Try these

- **Ray leap** — a ray arcs out of the water, turns, splashes with droplets + expanding ripples (with a splash sound).
- **Launch / Return kayak** — it bobs and cruises a full lap, then docks.
- **Tide** slider raises/lowers the water; the kayak, foam, and surface all follow.
- **Wind** drives wave height and tree/kelp/grass sway.
- **Time of day** smoothly morphs sky, sun/moon, fog, water tint — lanterns and cabin windows warm up toward night.
- **Viewpoints** glide between isometric, cabin/pier, and an underwater camera; **Free orbit** for exploration.
- **Music** starts on first click (calm pentatonic pad + plucks) with wind/wave ambience and a volume slider.

Everything is delta-time driven, capped pixel-ratio aware, responsive (panels collapse to a mobile bar), and built entirely from procedural geometry with `InstancedMesh` for the repeated details.
