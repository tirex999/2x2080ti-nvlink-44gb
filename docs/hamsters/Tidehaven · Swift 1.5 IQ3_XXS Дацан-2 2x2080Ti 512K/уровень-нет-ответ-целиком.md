function updateParticles(dt){

  }
}

## How it works & what to try

**The cutaway lagoon** — a glass box (front + left walls, top rim) holds a semi‑transparent turquoise water volume. The island pokes above the waterline, the front wall crops it into a clean cross‑section, and the seabed, rays, fish, plants, animated caustics and rising bubbles stay visible below. Render order + depth‑write rules keep the transparent surfaces from flickering.

**Controls**
- **View** — Isometric / Cozy / Underwater presets smoothly glide; **Free** hands control to OrbitControls.
- **Launch kayak** sends it circling the lagoon (with a wake); press again to return it to the pier.
- **Ray leap** — the hero ray arcs out of the water, turns, splashes down with droplets and expanding ripples.
- **Tide** slider raises/lowers the water; the surface, foam rings, kayak, splash level and ripples all track it.
- **Wind** drives wave amplitude, tree sway, sea‑grass motion, and the audio wind bed.
- **Time of day** cross‑fades sky, sun, fog, water tint, caustics, stars, and lights up cabin windows + lanterns at night.
- **Pause / Reset**, **Music** toggle, **Volume** slider.

**Audio** is fully procedural — a warm looping pad + arpeggio, filtered‑noise wind, and synthesized splashes — all gated behind the *Enter the diorama* click (autoplay‑safe).

**Technical** — delta‑time animation, capped DPR, reused geometry/materials, `InstancedMesh` fish schools, procedural canvas textures, responsive layout with collapsible panels on mobile.
