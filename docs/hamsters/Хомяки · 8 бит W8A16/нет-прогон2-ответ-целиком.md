function doWalk(h,dt){
  h.group.position.x+=Math.sin(h.heading)*h.speed*dt;
  h.group.position.z+=Math.cos(h.heading)*h.speed*dt;

  if(h.group.position.x<-CW/2+m){h.group.position.x=-CW/2+m;h.heading=angLerp(h.heading,Math.PI/2,0.4);}

}
function updateHamster(h,dt,t){

    case 'wheel':{

      h.wait-=dt;if(h.wait<=0){h.state='pause';h.wait=rand(1.5,3);}break;}
    case 'bowl':{

  h.group.rotation.x=Math.sin(h.wobble*0.7)*0.03*moving;

A few things worth knowing:

- **Everything is procedural** — no external models or textures, so it loads instantly and stays stable. The hamsters are built from flat‑shaded primitives (icosahedron body, sphere head/ears/eyes, little legs and a stubby tail) that squash, stretch, and bob while they scurry.
- **Behavior:** each hamster wanders, pauses to "snack," does a little spin, then picks a goal — the **wheel** (it actually spins faster when one's riding it), the **food bowl**, the **tunnel**, the **cheese wedge** (which gets nibbled), or the **carrot**. They also steer away from each other and the walls.
- **Interaction:** drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, toggle auto‑orbit, or use the +/− buttons. The right panel lists every resident with a live status, and toasts pop up when someone finds something.
